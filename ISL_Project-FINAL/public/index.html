How it works

A fluid experience from input to sign

1

Start a conversation

One person creates a conversation and shares the short code with the other.

2

Type or speak

The hearing person types or speaks naturally — full sentences, words, letters, or numbers.

3

Watch it signed

The other person watches a 3D avatar perform it in ISL, and can type a reply back.
